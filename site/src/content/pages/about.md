I like building things end to end, from algorithms and robots to full-stack apps, and I learn fastest by shipping them with a team.

I'm a third-year Computer Science student at Babeș-Bolyai University in Cluj-Napoca, studying in English. Most of my time goes into full-stack web work with Java and Spring Boot, Angular and React, with a soft spot for the lower-level stuff: interpreters, concurrency and making algorithms fast.

Before university there was robotics: two FTC national stages with my high school team, and later refereeing at the National Robotics Camp.

I speak Romanian (native), English (C1, Cambridge) and French (B1).
